Custom Phone Case Orders

Create a phone case that reflects your individuality. Vetroarts is developing precision UV-printing capabilities in Kampala to bring your photos, artwork, names, initials, and emblems to life.

Customisation Options

Ways to Personalise Your Case

We accommodate a range of creative specifications to match your exact visual preference.

Photographs

High-resolution prints of your cherished moments, family portraits, pets, or travel photography.

Artwork & Illustrations

Original graphic art, digital paintings, cultural patterns, and geometric prints.

Names & Initials

Clean typography, bespoke monograms, custom typography styles, or meaningful dates.

Logos & Insignia

Personal brand marks, club crests, community symbols, or creator emblems.

How It Works

The Custom Enquiry Process

Here is what to expect when you submit a custom order enquiry with Vetroarts.

1

Submit Details

Fill out the enquiry form below with your contact details, exact phone model, and design ideas.

2

Model Confirmation

Our team verifies mould availability for your phone model and confirms production feasibility.

3

Artwork Consultation

We review your artwork, image resolution, or text placement to ensure crisp UV print results.

4

Production Planning

Once you approve the digital preview, your custom piece is scheduled into our Kampala workflow.
