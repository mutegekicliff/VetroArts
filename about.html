Company Profile

About Vetroarts Accessories Ltd

A Ugandan phone-accessories enterprise based in Kampala, working to establish local manufacturing capability and deliver quality device protection for individuals and businesses across East Africa.

Our Background

Rooted in Kampala, Focused on Regional Need

Smartphones have become essential to everyday personal and professional life across East Africa. Yet most phone accessories sold in the regional market are imported with generic styling and limited options for local customisation or direct commercial partnerships.

Vetroarts Accessories Ltd was founded in Kampala to change that reality. We are developing the tooling, moulding, and high-definition UV-printing capability needed to produce phone cases locally, providing accessible protection tailored to African consumers and businesses.

By establishing production processes in Uganda, our goal is to shorten lead times for custom and commercial orders, offer responsive support, and foster regional industrial capacity.

Operational Status

Manufacturing in Development

Our production setup is currently under active development in Kampala. We are methodically testing mould configurations, setting up precision UV-print platforms, and establishing material supply chains.

Our Vision

Core Objectives

The principles guiding our development as we build local capabilities and serve our community.

Local Manufacturing

Developing local manufacturing capability in Kampala to assemble, print, and finish phone cases close to where our customers live and work.

East African Distribution

Our Product Focus

The Planned Product Range

Vetroarts is structuring its manufacturing pipeline around five versatile case categories:

  • 01

    Clear TPU, Silicone and Hybrid Cases

    Daily protection engineered with flexible polymers, tactile matte finishes, and dual-layer polycarbonate rims.

  • 02

    UV-Printed and Personalised Cases

    Custom-printed cases accommodating personal photos, digital illustrations, initials, and bespoke graphics.

  • 03

    Corporate-Branded Cases

    Branded cases prepared for businesses, institutions, and trade organisations for staff equipment and promotional merchandise.

  • 04

    MagSafe-Compatible Cases

    Cases embedded with magnetic arrays to support wireless charging alignment and magnetic phone accessories.

  • 05

    Carbon-Fibre Finish, Leather and Rugged Cases

    Speciality textures including technical carbon weave, classic stitched leather aesthetic, and heavy-duty armor cases.
