Trade & Enterprise

Business & Bulk Orders

Equip your workforce, delight your corporate clients, or stock your retail shelves. Vetroarts Accessories Ltd collaborates with companies, retailers, and institutions across Uganda and East Africa to deliver tailored case solutions.

Commercial Consultation & Capability Notice

Our local manufacturing facility in Kampala is actively under development. We review each trade enquiry individually to evaluate mould requirements, branding techniques, and scheduling feasibility. We do not enforce fixed order constraints; every proposal is assessed collaboratively with our partners.

How We Support Commercial Clients

From unified fleet protection to branded client merchandise, discover how phone cases support your organisation.

Company-Issued Staff Devices

Standardise the look and protection of company smartphones with durable cases featuring your corporate emblem and department badges.

Executive & Client Gifting

Retail & Distributor Supply

Our Approach

Commercial Engagement Process

We take a consultative approach to understanding your organisation's schedule and technical needs.

1

Enquiry Review

You share your organisation's estimated quantity, preferred case types, and branding guidelines.

2

Mould & Device Audit

We cross-reference your required device list against our active and scheduled Kampala mould tooling.

3

Digital Sample Approval

Our team prepares digital branding proofs showing exact logo alignment, pantone tones, and finishes.

4

Order Scheduling

We confirm manufacturing timelines, packaging options, and direct delivery to your specified location.
